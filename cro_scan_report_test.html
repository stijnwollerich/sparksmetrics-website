Summary

Executive summary

What is working

The homepage effectively showcases the Summit Sale, providing a clear invitation for users to shop both men's and women's items. The collection page offers extensive filtering options, promoting user engagement and tailored searching. The product page displays critical information concisely, enhancing user experience and enabling quicker decision-making.

What is hurting conversions

While the homepage has a strong visual impact, the CTA buttons lack contrast, making them less noticeable. The collection page features a cluttered layout with many options that could overwhelm users, leading to potential navigation issues. On the product page, the copy is dense, which could deter users from reading vital information and completing purchases.

Where the biggest opportunity is

Improving the visual hierarchy and clarity of key CTAs across all pages could significantly enhance conversion rates. Specifically, simplifying layout elements and employing more prominent CTAs can reduce friction and encourage customer action.

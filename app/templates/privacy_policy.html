Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Sparksmetrics (“we”, “our” or “us”) is a conversion rate optimization (CRO) and analytics agency for ecommerce brands. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with and what rights you have. It applies to our website sparksmetrics.com, our lead forms and tools, our services to clients, our YouTube channel and our use of YouTube API Services.

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Stijn – Wherever, trading as Sparksmetrics, Heuvel 23, 5492 AC Sint-Oedenrode, the Netherlands (VAT NL003475368B90). For any privacy question or request, email stijn@sparksmetrics.com.

When we work on a client’s store or analytics (see section 5), the client is the controller of the data in their systems and we act as their processor.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, company, website/store URL, phone number and anything else you enter in our forms (free CRO audit or scan, ebook download, qualification quiz, strategy session or call booking), or send us by email or chat.
  • Usage and device data: pages you visit, clicks, scrolling, referring site, campaign parameters, approximate location from your IP address, browser and device type, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).
  • Session recordings and heatmaps: we use Microsoft Clarity and Hotjar to understand how visitors use our website. These tools record interactions such as clicks, mouse movement and scrolling. Form fields are masked.
  • Client and billing data: contact details of the people we work with at client companies, contracts, invoices and payment details (payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers).
  • Data in client systems: when a client gives us access to their store, analytics, testing or advertising tools, we may see data about their visitors and customers (see section 5).

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To deliver what you asked for (the ebook, a CRO audit or scan, a booked call, a proposal) and to perform our contract with clients. Legal basis: contract or steps before a contract.
  • To follow up on your request and send relevant information about our services by email. You can unsubscribe at any time with one click. Legal basis: legitimate interest, or consent where the law requires it.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising (Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity and our own analytics). Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding and improving our marketing.
  • To run our business: invoicing, bookkeeping, security, fraud prevention and meeting legal obligations such as tax record keeping. Legal basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

We only share personal data with service providers that help us run our business, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Hosting and infrastructure: DigitalOcean (servers), Google Workspace (email and files).
  • Analytics and advertising: Google (Analytics, Ads, YouTube), Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads), Microsoft (Clarity), Hotjar.
  • Communication and scheduling: Brevo (email), Calendly (call booking), Slack (internal notifications about new requests).
  • Payments: Stripe.
  • Tools behind our free CRO scan: Browserless (loading and screenshotting the public pages of the store URL you submit) and OpenAI (analyzing those pages to write the report). We only send these providers the public pages of the URL you submit, not your contact details.
  • Authorities and advisers: when required by law, or to our accountant and legal advisers under confidentiality.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where that is the case, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Data we handle for our clients

For CRO and analytics work, clients give us access to tools such as Shopify, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, A/B testing tools and advertising accounts. Those tools can contain personal data about the client’s visitors and customers. For that data:

  • The client is the controller and we act as their processor. We only use the data to deliver the agreed services, on the client’s instructions.
  • We work with aggregated and pseudonymized data wherever possible, and we do not export customer lists unless the work requires it and the client agrees.
  • Access is limited to the team members working on the account and is removed when the engagement ends.
  • We never publish client data. Case studies and examples are anonymized unless the client has given written permission to be named.
  • A data processing agreement (DPA) is available on request.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to work properly, and to measure visits and ad performance: Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, Hotjar and our own analytics. Embedded YouTube videos may set cookies from Google when you play them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads through Google Ad Settings and Meta Ad Preferences. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

7. YouTube and YouTube API Services

We publish videos on our YouTube channel and embed YouTube videos on this website. We also use YouTube API Services through an internal tool, for one purpose only: uploading and scheduling videos on our own YouTube channel. This tool is used only by our team. It does not access, collect, store or share data about other YouTube users or channels.

  • By watching our videos, or using any feature that relies on YouTube, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • If you ever authorize one of our tools to access a YouTube or Google account, you can revoke that access at any time on the Google security settings page. We then delete any stored access tokens.

8. How long we keep data

  • Leads and enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client or ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Client and contract data: for the duration of the engagement and up to 24 months after, except where the law requires longer.
  • Invoices and bookkeeping: 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.
  • Data in client systems: we do not keep copies after the engagement ends. Our access is removed and any exports are deleted.
  • Analytics data: according to the retention settings of each tool (for Google Analytics, a maximum of 14 months).

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing (including for direct marketing), receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

To use any of these rights, email stijn@sparksmetrics.com. We respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority in your own country.

10. Security

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections, access limited to the people who need it, and removing access to client systems when an engagement ends. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep the risk low and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires it.

11. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or the law change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will let clients know directly.

13. Contact

Sparksmetrics (Stijn – Wherever) · Heuvel 23, 5492 AC Sint-Oedenrode, the Netherlands · stijn@sparksmetrics.com

See also our Terms of Service.
